NIGHT RELAY.

A courier, a camera drone, and a city filled with light. The craft, tools, and inspiration behind our imagined Samarkand festival.

01 / THE STORY

An imagined light festival

The courier's mural is commissioned within the fictional festival and painted on a freestanding panel. A friendly camera drone gives the signal to carry light through the night.

02 / CHARACTER & SOUND

Created with Higgsfield

The orange-and-plum female courier and natural movement samples were generated for this project through Higgsfield. Animation, wet footfalls, breathing, spray effects, musical signals, and the Pulse were assembled for the game.

Higgsfield ↗
03 / THE PLACE

Inspired by the Registan

Samarkand's architecture inspired the courtyards, arcades, and silhouettes. The scene uses procedural geometry and original mosaic patterns, shaped for a fictional game environment.
